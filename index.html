  1. Open this page from your computer in Chrome or Edge (the claude.ai viewer blocks USB and the mic): save it as bench/index.html, run python3 -m http.server in that folder, open localhost:8000.
  2. Put the bench program on the board: mpremote cp -r firmware/lib : then mpremote cp firmware/tests/09_bench_link/09_bench_link.py :main.py.
  3. Press Connect. The graph then shows what the board's own mic hears. Play a sound out loud next to the box to test it.
